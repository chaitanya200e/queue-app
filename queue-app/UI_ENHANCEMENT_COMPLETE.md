## 🚀 **Animation Details**

### **Page Entrance**
- Cards: `slideUp 0.7s` (staggered by 0.1s each)
- Titles: `slideDown 0.8s cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1)`
- Inputs: Fade + focus glow

### **Hover Effects**
- **Buttons**: `translateY(-4px) scale(1.05)` + enhanced shadow
- **Cards**: `translateY(-8px)` + glow effect
- **Tables**: `scale(1.01)` + background highlight
- **Slots**: `scale(1.02) translateY(-4px)`

### **Continuous Animations**
- **Gradient Background**: 15s infinite shift
- **Floating Overlay**: 20s ease-in-out float
- **Pulsing Cards**: 2s glow pulse
- **Token Panel**: Constant pulse glow
